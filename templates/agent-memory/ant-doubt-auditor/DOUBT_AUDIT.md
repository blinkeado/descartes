Source: agent-memory/data-reuse-discovery-ant/LOOP_LOG.md + FINDINGS.md

> Append-only, terse. One batch per audit. The auditor can only KILL or let a doubt SURVIVE —
> it never proposes one. A KILL cites a defect by number and the evidence that proves it;
> without named evidence the KILL is the auditor's own artefact and is withdrawn.
> SURVIVES means "no defect found" — never an endorsement of the science.
